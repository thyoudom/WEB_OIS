Learning Outcome-Based
Curriculum

Ontario International School’s curricular are the world’s most recent type
of curriculum named “Learning Outcome-Based Curriculum”. This curriculum
is designed to meet the developmental needs of the children.
Ontario International Schoolis committed to helping every child to achieve their
learning outcomes as indicated in our slogan 'Your Child Makes a Difference Every Day.' Every graduate from Ontario International School shall be a holistically developed child.

Whole Child Education Model

Ontario International School envisions holistically developed children who have the competencies (knowledge, skills and attitude) to Exercise, Explore, Expand, and Excel in their academic and real life.

Upon graduation from Ontario International School, each child will become:

A healthy child who has well-developed motor skills and five senses;

A critical thinker who is curious, courageous, and creative;

An effective communicator who expresses their ideas and feelings effectively; and

A good citizen who connects, shares and cares for others.

Toddler Class ( 1 - 3 years )

Ontario International School provides not only excellent care services
but also appropriate educational program to toddlers aged from 1 to 3 years.

1. The teachers have a degree in early childhood education.

2. The teachers and staff are gentle and care for children’s wellbeing.

3. Train children how to use toilets appropriately.

4. Build a strong foundation of English language.

5. Use special teaching methods to develop children’s ability to recognize letters and words quickly and be able to speak at an early age

6. Conduct learning activities to help children to maintain balance when they move or stand

7. Strategies to help children to hold a pencil properly

8. Integrate fun in all learning activities making children happy all day

9. Create fun through story telling that have good educational morals

10. Encourage parents to help develop a learning habit at home

11. Encourage infants to eat and sleep well that would lead to a healthy body and healthy mind

Kindergarten (3 - 5 years old)


Ontario International School provides not only excellent care services but also appropriate educational program to kindergarten pupils aged from 3 to 5 years.

1)  The teachers have a degree in early childhood education.
2)  The teachers and staff are gentle and care for children’s wellbeing.
3)  Use Learning Outcome-Based Curriculum from Canada and
     Ministry of Education, Youth and Sport
4)  Focus on holistic development (physical, cognitive, socio-emotional,
      and communication development)
5)  Provide unique programs to ensure holistic development (yoga, meditation, home         economics, swimming, arts, music and songs, craft, math, social sciences, language,       computer, etc.
6)  Conduct a free play program to identify the children’s innate orientation
7)  Check the child’s temperature 3 times a day and weight them 1 time a week
8)  Provide a detailed daily record to parents about the child’s temperature, and eating       (e.g. amount of rice and amount of food they eat, amount of water, amount of milk)
9)  Children quickly learn to speak, write and read.
10) Extra-curricular activities connect the lessons learned in class with real experiences
11)  Children become brave persons.
12)  Children learn to be independent and responsible for their own actions such as eating, dressing, and tidying the learning materials after play.
13)  Teachers and teacher assistants encourage children to eat enough and properly that could make the children healthy body and healthy mind.
