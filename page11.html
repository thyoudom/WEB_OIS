FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

 Are there any security cameras on your school campus?

There are security cameras all over the building to ensure complete security of the children. Parents can request to watch the video camera on campus at any time?

What extra curricular activities do you offer?

Children and parents are encouraged to join at least four study tours per year. The purposes of the study tours are to provide the children opportunities to socialize with their friends, other children, adults, and the community in order to raise awareness and build social skills. Through these programs, the children will also explore the real world and construct their knowledge. This practice aligns with the Progressivism Philosophy of Education.

What are your teachers qualifications?

All international teachers have Master's degree in education and many years experiences in teaching. All Khmer teachers have Bachelor's degrees and professional certificates in teaching recognized by the Ministry of Education, Youth and Sport.

What language is used as a medium of instruction?

Two languages are used as media of instruction: English for the international program and Khmer for the national program. Chinese is also taught in a part-time class every day.
